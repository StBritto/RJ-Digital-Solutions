# Splytto Store Listing Draft

**App name:** Splytto

**Short description:** Split expenses, scan bills with AI, settle with UPI, and turn trips into shareable stories.

**Full description:**
Splytto helps friends, teams and travellers split everyday expenses without the awkward maths. Create a split, add people, scan a receipt, assign line items, track balances and settle through your preferred UPI payment app.

Splytto goes beyond expense splitting with Stories: attach photos, places and notes to expenses, build a trip recap, and share memories to social apps. Friends can receive a smart share link to view what they owe, even before they install Splytto.
